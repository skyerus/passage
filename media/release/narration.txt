Do you read on a Kindle and an Xteink?

You want to pick up where you left off on either reader, and have somewhere to find the highlights you've made on both.

Passage is a free, open-source Mac app built for that.

Highlight in KOReader or a supported CrossPoint reader. Passage collects your quotes into one searchable library, with book covers and the dates your reader records.

Search for a passage, then copy just the quote or include its book and author.

If you only use a Kindle, you can still keep your highlights in Passage. Just import its My Clippings file.

For reading progress, put the same EPUB on both readers. On CrossPoint, use Upload Local and Apply Remote to move your position between them.

Your library stays on your Mac, with backups to iCloud Drive or a folder you choose. You don't need paid hosting or a GitHub account.

Live Kindle syncing needs a supported jailbreak and KOReader. CrossPoint needs Passage's matching firmware. Your Mac must be awake and reachable.

Passage is in public beta, with setup instructions and all the source code on GitHub.
