You bought a second e-reader. So why does your reading life have to start over?

One book on your Kindle. Another reader in your pocket. And the line you wanted to remember? On the wrong device.

Meet Passage. A free, open-source Mac app that brings your reading together.

Highlight in KOReader or a supported CrossPoint reader. Passage collects your quotes into one searchable library, with book covers and the dates your reader records.

Find a favourite line. Copy it on its own, or take the book and author with it.

Already have years of Kindle highlights? Import your My Clippings file. One reader is enough to get started.

Reading progress can travel too. Use the same EPUB on both readers, with manual upload and apply controls on CrossPoint.

Your library lives on your Mac. Back up to iCloud Drive or a folder you choose. No hosting bill. No GitHub account needed to use it.

For live syncing, Kindle needs a supported jailbreak and KOReader. CrossPoint needs Passage's matching firmware. Your Mac must be awake and reachable.

Passage is in public beta. Download it. Explore the code. Make it your own.

Different readers. One place for the words worth keeping.
